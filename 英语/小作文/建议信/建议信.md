
### 模版一：正式文体（致机构/领导/教授）

> [!info] 适用场景
> 
> 写给图书馆馆长、校长、餐厅经理、书店老板等。语气需要**委婉、客气、客观**。
> 
>   

**Dear `[Name/Title, e.g., Mr. President / Sir or Madam]`,**

  
**① 表明写信目的（破题）：**

I am writing to express my continuous interest in `[相关领域，如：our university library]`, and I would like to offer a few practical suggestions to further improve `[你想改进的事物，如：the study environment]`.

  

**② 提出具体建议（2-3点，使用连接词和委婉语气）：**

**First and foremost, I highly recommend that** `[建议1，如：the opening hours should be extended, especially during the exam week]`. **In addition, it would be highly beneficial if you could** `[建议2，如：upgrade the campus network system]`. **Last but not least, I sincerely believe that** `[建议3/期望结果，如：taking these measures will greatly enhance students' learning efficiency]`.

  

**③ 结尾客套与期待：**

I hope you will find these proposals helpful and take them into careful consideration. Your prompt attention to this matter would be highly appreciated.

  

**Yours sincerely,**

**Li Ming**

  

### 模版二：半正式文体（致朋友/同学）

> [!info] 适用场景
> 
> 朋友向你咨询如何学好一门外语、如何适应大学生活、或者你建议朋友参加某个活动。语气可以**真诚、热情、直接**。
> 
>   

**Dear `[Friend's Name]`,**

  

**① 表明写信目的（破题）：**

I am so glad to receive your letter asking for my advice on `[朋友咨询的事情，如：how to prepare for the postgraduate entrance exam]`. From my perspective, the following suggestions might be of some help to you.

  

**② 提出具体建议（祈使句或情态动词为主）：**

**To begin with, you'd better** `[建议1，如：make a detailed and reasonable study schedule]`. **Furthermore, it is advisable for you to** `[建议2，如：focus on core subjects like Data Structures and Operating Systems]`. **More importantly, never underestimate the power of** `[建议3/某种精神，如：persistence and daily practice]`.

  

**③ 结尾客套与期待：**

I hope you will find these suggestions practical and useful. Please feel free to contact me if you have any further questions. Best wishes for your success!

  

**Yours sincerely,**

**Li Ming**
